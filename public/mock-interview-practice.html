Why Generic Interview Prep Fails

📚

Reading guides isn't practice

Knowing the theory of how to answer "tell me about yourself" is different from being able to say it confidently under pressure. Reading is passive. You need to speak.

🤝

Human practice is hard to arrange

Asking friends to interview you only works a few times before it gets awkward. Professional coaches are expensive. AI practice is available at midnight the night before your interview.

🎯

Generic questions miss the point

A software engineer and a marketing manager need completely different practice. Questions tailored to your actual CV and target role are the ones you will face in the room.

AI mock interview practice uses artificial intelligence to simulate a real job interview. The AI asks questions based on your CV and target role, listens to your answers, and gives instant feedback on your content, communication, pacing, and confidence.

It's different. A friend can give gut reactions; AI gives structured, consistent feedback you can act on. AI practice is also available at any hour and never gets bored after the third repetition — which matters when you are trying to make your answers feel natural.
