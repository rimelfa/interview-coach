What Your AI Coach Analyses

Answer content

Did you actually answer the question? Did you use a clear structure? Was the story specific and credible? The AI assesses whether your answer would satisfy an experienced interviewer.

Communication clarity

Were your sentences clear and direct? Did you ramble or repeat yourself? Did you use confident language or hedge everything? The AI identifies patterns in how you communicate.

Pacing and filler words

In voice mode, the AI listens for filler words (um, uh, like, you know), speaking pace, and whether your answer stayed within an appropriate length for the question type.

A better version of your answer

For every answer you give, the AI shows you what a stronger version looks like — not a generic template, but a rewrite of your actual content, improved.

Questions About AI Coaching

Is AI coaching as good as working with a human interview coach?
For repetition and building confidence through practice, AI coaching is often more effective — because you can do it any time and as many times as needed. Human coaches are better for subjective impression management and career strategy. Most candidates get the most value from using both: AI for daily practice, human coaching for targeted feedback before a final round.
What interview questions does the AI ask?
The AI generates questions based on your CV and the job description you provide. It covers behavioural questions, role-specific technical questions, CV walkthrough questions, and competency questions. The more context you give it, the more relevant the questions.
Can I practise in voice mode?
Yes. Voice mode lets you speak your answers out loud, which is closer to the real interview experience. The AI transcribes and analyses your spoken answer, including filler words and pacing. Text mode is also available if you prefer to type.
